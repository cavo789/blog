> /ask Why does VID_2044.mp4 get its date from the file and not from exiftool?

exiftool returned no DateTimeOriginal and no CreateDate for that file. Phones write the
recording date in the QuickTime CreateDate tag, but many editors and messaging apps strip
it when they re-encode or export a video. Get-ExifDates finds nothing, so Export-PhotoList
falls back to the file creation date and sets DateSource to "file".
