# Service Agreement

This agreement is entered into between **Acme Consulting SRL** ("the Provider") and **Nordwind Logistics NV** ("the Client").

## Scope of Work

The Provider will deliver software consulting services for a period of 6 months, starting January 15, 2027.

## Fees

The agreed daily rate is **EUR 650**, invoiced monthly in arrears.

## Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all exchanged information confidential for a period of 3 years after the termination of this agreement.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 calendar days' written notice.
