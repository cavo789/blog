According to your notes, you configured the VPN using Tailscale. The steps you took were:

1. Installed the Tailscale client on both the home PC and the work laptop.
2. Ran `tailscale up` on each machine and authenticated with the shared Tailscale account.
3. Noted the Tailscale IP assigned to the home PC, usually in the `100.x.y.z` range.

When you're off the home LAN, you need to replace any references to `192.168.0.218` (the
original home LAN address) with the Tailscale IP whenever using local tooling on your work
laptop.
